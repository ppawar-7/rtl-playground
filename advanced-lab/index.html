🧭 Interactive Datapath & ALU Guide: Multi-bit buses group wires together into words (e.g., 4-bit nibbles). Click input vector bits (A, B) below to change numerical values. Select an ALU operation (ADD, SUB, AND, OR, XOR) to observe zero-cycle combinational calculation and live arithmetic condition flags: Z (Zero), C (Carry Out), V (Two's Complement Overflow), and N (Negative).
🧭 Sequential Systems Guide: Registers store binary words across clock cycles. Toggle the Enable and Reset switches, then click "Single Clock Pulse" to advance time. Notice how data is captured strictly on the 0→1 rising clock transition! Check the Waveforms tab to view multi-bit bus activity.
🧭 Finite State Machine (FSM) Guide: FSMs are the control brains of digital chips. Select a state preset (e.g. 101 Sequence Detector or Traffic Controller), toggle input bits, and step the clock to trace active states in the interactive diagram. Compare Moore outputs (registered, glitch-free) with Mealy outputs (combinational, zero-latency).
